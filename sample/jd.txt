Senior Software Engineer, Payments Infrastructure — Orbit Commerce (fictional)

The Payments Infrastructure team builds the systems that move money for millions of merchants. We own payment orchestration, ledgering, webhooks, and the reliability of every transaction path.

What you'll do

What you'll bring
5+ years building backend systems in Python, Go, or Java.
Experience with distributed messaging such as Kafka and with idempotent API design.
A track record of owning production systems and improving their latency and reliability.
Clear written communication; you can turn ambiguity into a plan.
Experience with ledgering or double-entry accounting systems is a plus.
